## Steps
1. Add an entry to docs/research/graveyard.md in its existing format: date, idea, what was tried, which gate failed and the numbers, registry link, and what would have to change to retry.
2. Make sure the failed runs are in the experiment registry.
3. Never delete or edit past entries; append only.
