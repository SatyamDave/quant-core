# Walk-forward: demo (2026-09-27)

## Assumptions

- Data: synthetic day from `data/recorders/synthetic.py` (seed 7, 60000 events, 2026-01-05). Not market data; results say nothing about any venue.
- Fills: `taker_at_touch`. Every signal takes the touch at the signal event and exits at the touch 50 events later. Queue position and partial fills are not modeled.
- Costs: half the spread at entry and at exit, plus taker fee 5.0 bps on each leg. Funding is not applied (holds last seconds). Slippage beyond the touch is not modeled.
- Latency: configured (`constant`, order entry 1500000 ns, market data 500000 ns) but not simulated; signals act on the same event.
- Sharpe is per trade opportunity (one every 50 events, non-overlapping), not annualized.
- Folds: 5 expanding walk-forward folds, purged by the 50-event label horizon and embargoed 50 events; first 20000 samples train only.
- Trials: deflated Sharpe uses the cumulative registry count for `demo` (4), failures included.
