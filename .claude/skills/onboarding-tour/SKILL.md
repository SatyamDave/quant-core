## Steps
1. Read the root CLAUDE.md and docs/VISION.md; summarize the mission and the 12 rules in plain words.
2. Walk the repo map: engine/, strategies/, research/, ml/, backtest/, data/, agents/, config/, ops/, docs/. Point out protected zones.
3. Show the commands: `just setup`, `just check`, `just test`, `just replay`.
4. Explain the lifecycle gates (strategies/_template/GATES.md) and the knowledge loop (.claude/learnings/).
5. Point to docs/onboarding/ for day-1 and week-1 tasks.
